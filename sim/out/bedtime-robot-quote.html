How to read this

  • Landed cost = parts + printing + labour + setup + courier + warranty reserve + returns, per order the customer keeps (blueprint §8.2).
  • Price to charge is the higher of: 30% gross margin at the typical (p50) cost, or 10% margin even at the bad-case (p95) cost, after the payment-gateway fee, rounded up to ₹…99 and shown with 18% GST.
  • Cross-check: an Indian print bureau at ₹3–5/cm³ (IamRapid, published rates) would charge about ₹301–₹501 for the two printed parts.
  • Inputs: sim/spec/catalog.json (blueprint §3.3 ranges), sim/spec/assumptions.json (every guess labelled), sim/out/slice-stats.json (slicer output).
