How it works

From an idea to your hands, and beyond.

You bring the idea. We do the engineering, the building and the shipping.

  1. Imagine

    Describe the physical product you want

    Plain words are enough. We ask a couple of questions and show you concepts.

  2. Engineer

    AI designs hardware, enclosure, and editable software

    You see the shell in 3D, the parts inside it and a delivered price, before you order.

  3. Manufacture

    Build, flash firmware, verify, and ship

    We print the shell, fit the parts, load the software and check that each part works before it ships.

  4. Receive

    Unbox it, switch it on, connect it

    Plug it in, then connect it to your home Wi-Fi from your phone.

  5. Modify anytime

    Talk to your app, Claude, or another supported AI client

    Ask for a new story, a new light or a whole new job, in plain words.

  6. Deploy and repurpose

    Validate, update, verify, and roll back if needed

    A new version runs on trial first. If it fails its checks, the gadget goes back to the last good one by itself.

Questions

Good to know before you start.

Do I need to know electronics?

No. You describe what you want in plain words. We work out the parts, the shell and the software, then build it for you.

If you like to tinker, you can also download the parts list, the build guide and the design files.

What does it cost?

You see one delivered price before you order: parts, printing, assembly, testing, courier and GST, all in. For example, ₹7,826 (illustrative).

For now every price is an estimate until our team reviews the design, and nothing is charged or reserved. The bedtime robot runs on a USB-C phone charger, which is not in the box.

Can I change it later?

Yes. Ask for the change in plain words, in our app, in Claude or in another supported AI client.

A new version runs on trial first. If it fails its start-up checks, the gadget goes back to the last good version by itself. Saved favourites are kept apart, so updates do not erase them.

Where do you ship?

India first. Prices are in rupees and include courier within India. The delivery window is an estimate until we know your pincode.

Is it safe for kids?

Designs made for children get an extra safety and privacy review before we confirm a price. An adult sets the gadget up, with a parent PIN.

In the bedtime robot, the microphone is on only while the button is held and the light is blue. Her voice clips stay on the memory card inside the robot. Nothing is uploaded.
